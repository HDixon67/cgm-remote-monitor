                    How do I earn XP?
                    • 🍽️ Log a meal: +10 (up to 5 a day) · 💉 a dose: +5 · 💊 a med: +5 · 🩸 a period day: +15
                    • ⏱️ Each hour mostly in range: +2 · 🌤️ Good day (70%+): +25 · 🌟 Great day (85%+): +25 more
                    • 😴 In range all night: +20
                    • 🦸 Low back up within 30 min: +20 · 🧘 no rebound high after: +15

                    • XP never goes down. Highs and lows never cost you anything.
                    • 🔥 Streak = days you logged something. Every 7 days earns a ❄️ freeze that covers a missed day.

                      How are phases worked out?

                      From the period start dates you log. Period = your logged period days (or 5 days). Ovulation ≈ 14 days before your next period (± 1 day). Follicular = between them. Luteal = after ovulation until your next period. These are estimates, not medical tests.

                      Patterns here are for talking with your care team. Don’t change doses based on this alone.

                      Connect to Nightscout

                      How do I get a token?
                      1. Open your Nightscout site and log in.
                      2. ☰ menu → Admin Tools → Add new Subject.
                      3. Name: cgmhub. Roles: readable and careportal. Save.
                      4. Copy the token next to it (it starts with cgmhub-) and paste it here.

                      The token stays on this phone only. Don’t use your API_SECRET here.

                      📥 Import Libre history

                      How to get your file from LibreView
                      1. On a computer, go to libreview.com and sign in with your LibreLink account.
                      2. Click Glucose History (top right) → Download glucose data.
                      3. Complete the captcha → Download. You get a .csv file.
                      4. Send it to your phone (email, Google Drive) or open this page on the computer.
                      Before you import: give your token upload permission
                      1. Nightscout ☰ → Admin Tools → Add new Role. Name: import. Permissions: api:entries:create. Save.
                      2. Edit your cgmhub subject and add the import role.
                      3. After importing, you can remove that role again.

                      ❤️ Health Connect

                      📱 Home-screen widget

                      In the CGM Hub Android app: long-press your home screen → Widgets → CGM Hub, then drag it on. It shows your number, arrow, change, insulin on board and your top side quest or next med. The colour shows low, in range or high, and it greys out if the reading is old.

                      It updates live while CGM Hub is open, and about every 15 minutes in the background. Android doesn’t allow widgets to refresh more often than that. Tap it to open the app and get the latest.

                      🥗 Nutrition

                      This is what gets logged and used by the bolus calculator and forecast. Ask your care team which way they want you to count.

                      Daily targets

                      Standard adult Daily Values to start. Change them to what your dietitian recommends; leave blank to use the default. Calories and carbs have no default.

                      🍎 Food search

                      Food search uses USDA FoodData Central (plain foods + brands). Barcode scans use Open Food Facts. The shared key only allows a few searches an hour, so get your own free key:

                      1. Open fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-key-signup, enter your name + email.
                      2. Copy the key from the email and paste it here.

                      🔔 Phone reminders

                      Sent to the Pushover app every 15 minutes when something is due, and only if you haven’t already logged it.

                      One-time setup (Pushover)
                      1. Install Pushover on your phone and create an account (30-day free trial, then a one-time purchase).
                      2. On pushover.net, copy your User Key. Then Create an Application/API Token (name it “CGM Hub”) and copy its API Token.
                      3. In Northflank → project nightcloud → Secrets → nightscout, add PUSHOVER_API_TOKEN and PUSHOVER_ALARM_KEY (your User Key). Save.
                      4. Come back here and tap Send a test.

                      The same keys also turn on Nightscout’s low/high alarms on your phone.
