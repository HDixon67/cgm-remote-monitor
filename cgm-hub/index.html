                    How do I earn XP?
                    • 🍽️ Log a meal: +10 (up to 5 a day) · 💉 a dose: +5 · 💊 a med: +5 · 🩸 a period day: +15
                    • ⏱️ Each hour mostly in range: +2 · 🌤️ Good day (70%+): +25 · 🌟 Great day (85%+): +25 more
                    • 😴 In range all night: +20
                    • 🦸 Low back up within 30 min: +20 · 🧘 no rebound high after: +15
                    • 👋 Daily check-in: +5 · ⚔️ quests: +30 each
                    • XP never goes down. Highs and lows never cost you anything.
                    • 🔥 Streak = days you logged something. Every 7 days earns a ❄️ freeze that covers a missed day.

                    How are phases worked out?

                    From the period start dates you log. Period = your logged period days (or 5 days). Ovulation ≈ 14 days before your next period (± 1 day). Follicular = between them. Luteal = after ovulation until your next period. These are estimates, not medical tests.

                    Patterns here are for talking with your care team. Don’t change doses based on this alone.

                    Connect to Nightscout

                    How do I get a token?
                    1. Open your Nightscout site and log in.
                    2. ☰ menu → Admin Tools → Add new Subject.
                    3. Name: cgmhub. Roles: readable and careportal. Save.
                    4. Copy the token next to it (it starts with cgmhub-) and paste it here.

                    The token stays on this phone only. Don’t use your API_SECRET here.

                    📥 Import Libre history

                    How to get your file from LibreView
                    1. On a computer, go to libreview.com and sign in with your LibreLink account.
                    2. Click Glucose History (top right) → Download glucose data.
                    3. Complete the captcha → Download. You get a .csv file.
                    4. Send it to your phone (email, Google Drive) or open this page on the computer.
                    Before you import: give your token upload permission
                    1. Nightscout ☰ → Admin Tools → Add new Role. Name: import. Permissions: api:entries:create. Save.
                    2. Edit your cgmhub subject and add the import role.
                    3. After importing, you can remove that role again.
